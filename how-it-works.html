How it works

  1. Open the deals page. We request your location so nearby offers can be sorted by distance.
  2. Browse by category, area, or distance. Each card shows the offer, validity, and area.
  3. Call the business and confirm the deal before you visit.
  4. Use Maps for directions. Share a deal with family using the Share button.

Distances are an estimate. Google Maps shows the driving route when you open directions.
